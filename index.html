Flow anomalies — this week vs. each flow's own 90-day weekly average

Every live flow with meaningful volume, compared against itself over the trailing 90 days (divided into weekly averages) — not against a generic industry benchmark. Flows sending under ~15 recipients/week aren't included; at that volume, one order swings the numbers too much to call anything an anomaly.

Four flows — Welcome series, Abandoned Added to Cart, Browse Abandonment, and Customer Thank You — converted $0 this week despite normal-to-above-normal send volume. Worth a look, but worth some caution too: each of these flows only converts a handful of orders in a typical week even at full health, so one slow week can look identical to a broken automation. Known data gaps (not hidden)
QuickBooks' P&L tool did not return COGS/OpEx/Net Income this pull (row-cap issue on a 2,500+ item chart of accounts) — Gross Margin/Net Margin cards use the last confirmed monthly figures instead of this week's. Neither Amazon nor Shopify's connected tools expose a star-rating total, review count, or CS response-time metric. Walmart has no published account-health/suspension-risk scorecard through this connection — only connection status is shown, not a fabricated health score. Flow anomaly deltas use each flow's own 90-day weekly average as the baseline, not a statistical significance test — small-volume flows are flagged as such rather than called "broken." Zendesk CSAT: no data — surveys not configured to go out, not a reflection of customer satisfaction.
