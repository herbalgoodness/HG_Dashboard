# Weekly refresh — what every tab needs

`index.html` is a static, self-contained file. Nothing in it fetches live data;
it is regenerated by two scheduled Claude Routines that commit a new
`index.html` to `main`:

| Routine | When (UTC) | Touches |
|---|---|---|
| Herbal Goodness Command Center — weekly refresh | Mon 13:00 (8am Central) | Every tab except the PPC Action Plan block |
| Herbal Goodness Weekly Amazon Ads PPC Review | Sun 20:03 | Only the "PPC Action Plan — Amazon Ads" block on Overview |

The Monday refresh must leave the PPC Action Plan block exactly as the Sunday
job left it.

### Operations tab: what to rebuild each week

- Eyebrow line: `Amazon FBA (agentcentral) · Shopify · live pull <date>`.
- **Amazon — zero fulfillable inventory**: active products at 0 fulfillable FBA
  units, with reserved, inbound, and coverage status. Deduplicate SKU aliases,
  stickerless variants, and reimbursement placeholders.
- **Amazon — critical / high stockout risk**: days of supply =
  (fulfillable + inbound) ÷ trailing-30-day velocity. Show AWD units when they
  would cover the gap.
- **Amazon — aged 180+ day, excess, and unfulfillable**: the 180+ day bucket by
  units, the top LTSF $ exposure, and overstock flags such as zero sales in 30
  days.
- **Shopify — zero inventory**, including cross-channel stockouts (same SKU out
  on both channels).
- **Top 10 recommended actions**, rewritten from this week's findings.
- **IPI Management Plan** (target 800): no API exposes the IPI score. Keep the
  last manually checked score with its "as of" date unless a fresh source
  returns one. Never invent a new score. Rebuild the P1/P2/P3 pillar bullets
  from this week's data.

### Projects tab: what to rebuild each week

- Eyebrow line: `Asana · Herbal Goodness workspace · live pull <date>`.
- **Overdue tasks by member**: @herbalgoodnessco.com members, excluding shared
  inboxes (for example, HG Warehouse) and external contractors. List those
  separately.
- **Board summaries** for the 7 core boards: CEO Board, Company Issues & TODO,
  Pillars 1–4, and Company Rocks Board. Show the owner, the open-task count, and
  a short note on each.
- **Company Rocks Board, current quarter**: average "% Rock % Accomplished" by
  department. Leave blanks out of the average (don't count them as 0), and list
  off-track rocks.
- **Reporting gaps**: IPI is a manual source, excess-inventory definitions
  differ, Asana caps results at 100 rows, and the guest split is inferred from
  email domain.

## Dates

`const DATA_PULL_DATE` and `const NEXT_REFRESH_LABEL` in the `<script>` block
drive the header chip and the footer date. Update both on every run. The two
per-tab eyebrow dates above must match `DATA_PULL_DATE`.
